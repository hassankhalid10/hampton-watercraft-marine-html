Our Inspection Process

To ensure we maintain the highest standards for our inventory and offer you the most accurate value, every boat undergoes a comprehensive Hampton Certified evaluation. Our factory-trained technicians inspect:

  • Mechanical Systems: Full diagnostic on engines, drives, and fuel systems.
  • Structural Integrity: Detailed hull, deck, and fiberglass assessment.
  • On-Board Electronics: Testing of navigation, audio, and electrical components.
  • Cosmetic Condition: Evaluation of upholstery, gelcoat, and canvas.
