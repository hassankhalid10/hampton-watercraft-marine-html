Mercury Marine
Service Award

Hampton Watercraft & Marine is proud to have received the prestigious 2025 Mercury Marine Service Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) Award on behalf of both our Hampton Bays and Eastport service teams. This award recognizes dealerships that provide exceptional service and support for Mercury outboards and MerCruiser engines based on post-service customer surveys that evaluate satisfaction, service quality, and overall customer experience.

This marks the sixth consecutive year that Hampton Watercraft & Marine has been awarded this prestigious recognition. We are proud to celebrate setting this standard for customer satisfaction in the marine industry.

Our 75 Ton Marine Travel Lift

Our heavy-duty 75-ton marine travel lift is one of the largest on Long Island, enabling safe haul-out, launch, and storage for yachts and large vessels up to 82 feet.

Convenient Mobile Boat Service Across Eastern Long Island

From routine maintenance to emergency repairs, our mobile marine service team delivers expert care directly to your dock or marina across Eastern Long Island. Whether your boat is moored in the Hamptons, North Fork, or anywhere along the East End, our certified technicians come equipped to handle service calls so you can spend more time on the water.
