Mercury V12 Certified

When your boat needs attention, certified expertise makes all the difference. Our technicians are certified by the National Marine Electronics Association (NMEA) and by Mercury outboards, MerCruiser, Crusader inboards, and Volvo inboards & stern drives. We pair those credentials with rigorous quality checks to deliver consistently reliable service. Our unmatched approach aims for precision, communication, and attention to detail, so your boat spends less time in the shop and more time on the water.

Our Mercury-certified marine technicians are specially trained to service and diagnose today's advanced Mercury Verado® outboard systems, including the powerful 7.6L V12 600hp platform. Led by V12 System Lab-certified technicians Rob Waller (Eastport) and Ernie Miller (Hampton Bays), our service departments provide expert support for high-horsepower marine engines across Eastern Long Island. Backed by the Mercury Marine CSI Award for six years straight, our service team is committed to delivering exceptional workmanship and customer care across every vessel we service and every customer we support.

Hampton Watercraft & Marine is proud to have received the prestigious 2025 Mercury Marine Service Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) Award on behalf of both our Hampton Bays and Eastport service teams. This award recognizes dealerships that provide exceptional service and support for Mercury outboards and MerCruiser engines based on post-service customer surveys that evaluate satisfaction, service quality, and overall customer experience.

This marks the sixth consecutive year that Hampton Watercraft & Marine has been awarded this prestigious recognition. We are proud to celebrate setting this standard for customer satisfaction in the marine industry.
