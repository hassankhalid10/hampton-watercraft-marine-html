Your Complete Pro Shop

Our Eastport, NY Pro Shop is fully stocked with boating supplies, watersports gear, engine parts, boat cleaning products, marine accessories, and on-the-water essentials. If we don’t have the item you need in stock, we’re happy to special order it.

We carry Hyperlite wakeboards, HO life vests, water skis, tubes, kneeboards, surfboards, Perception Sport kayaks, Body Glove and BOTE paddle boards, plus a wide selection of bait & tackle for Long Island fishing.
