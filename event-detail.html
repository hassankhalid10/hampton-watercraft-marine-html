About

Join Bonita Boat Center for an exclusive Regulator VIP Event in Naples, Florida—a private, RSVP-only showcase featuring four of Regulator Marine’s most sought-after models: the 26XO, 28, 31, and the all-new 35. This immersive experience offers attendees the opportunity to explore these exceptional boats up close, engage with our knowledgeable team, and enjoy personalized sea trials. Whether you’re a seasoned boater or new to the water, this event provides a unique chance to discover the performance, craftsmanship, and innovation that define the Regulator brand. Spaces are limited for this exclusive gathering. Secure your spot today and take the first step toward your next adventure on the water.
