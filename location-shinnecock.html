Premier Boat Sales

Our Shinnecock location features a diverse on-water showroom where you can experience the latest models in their element. We are proud authorized dealers for:

  • Luxury & Offshore: Tiara Yachts, Tiara Sport
  • Fishing & Family: Boston Whaler, Key West Boats, Rossiter
  • Towboats & Tenders: MasterCraft, Brig USA
  • Personal Watercraft: Yamaha WaveRunners

Marina & Dockage Services

With over 100 slips and a deep-water basin, we accommodate everything from nimble runabouts to large luxury yachts:

  • In-Water Slips: Seasonal and transient dockage for vessels up to 82 feet.
  • Valet Services: Premium high-and-dry valet service for instant access to the water.
  • Winter Storage: Expansive, secure winter storage with shrink-wrapping and climate-controlled options.
  • Elite Service Department: The only Mercury Marine dealer in NY to receive the Mercury CSI Award for excellence.
