Storage Options

At Hampton Watercraft & Marine

Hampton Watercraft & Marine provides secure short-term and long-term boat storage solutions designed to protect your vessel during the off-season and beyond. From winter storage to seasonal marina accommodations, our professional storage services help keep your investment protected year-round.

Request Storage Storage Contract

Ways To Store Your Boat

In-Water Slip Dockage

Ideal for frequent users with floating and fixed docks offering direct access to the water.

High-and-Dry Valet / Rack Storage

Our launch-on-demand service keeps your boat protected from the elements when you aren’t using it.

Outdoor Storage

Secure on-land storage options complete with winterization and shrink-wrapping services.

Personal Watercraft (PWC) Storage

Convenient drive-on docks and dry spaces specifically designed for WaveRunners.
