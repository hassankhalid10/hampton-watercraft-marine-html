Hampton Watercraft & Marine proudly operates three on-water marina facilities on Long Island, New York to better serve boaters across the East End. With locations on the Shinnecock Canal, Moriches Bay, and Smith Creek in Hampton Bays, our marinas offer premium boating services, top marine brands, new and used boats for sale, and professional boat brokerage services to help owners buy or sell quality pre-owned boats.

For over 50 years, Tiara Yachts has set the standard for American luxury yachts with meticulously crafted vessels designed for performance, elegance, and comfort. Featuring advanced technology, spacious social zones, and premium finishes, Tiara yachts are built to elevate every boating experience.

Recognized as “The Unsinkable Legend,” Boston Whaler is one of the most trusted names in boating. Boston Whaler boats feature unsinkable construction, advanced technology, and premium amenities for every day on the water, ensuring reliable performance in every ride.

From inshore fishing to offshore adventures, Key West Boats offers dependable center console and bay boat models designed for performance, durability, and a welcome refuge from life’s daily grind.

NAVAN Boats offers a modern approach to luxury boating with sleek, adventure-ready vessels designed for exploration, entertaining, and overnight cruising. Combining advanced digital features with premium craftsmanship, NAVAN delivers a refined boating experience for today’s lifestyle-focused boater.

BRIG is a global leader in rigid inflatable boats (RIBs), combining military-grade engineering with luxury styling, advanced technology, and exceptional on-water performance. Designed for stability, speed, and versatility, BRIG boats are ideal for coastal cruising, yacht tender applications, and family adventures.

Yamaha WaveRunners deliver industry-leading performance, reliability, and innovation for riders seeking excitement on the water. Featuring advanced RiDE® technology, powerful Yamaha marine engines, and comfortable rider-focused designs, WaveRunners are built for watersports, cruising, and family fun.

Service Centers

Hampton Watercraft & Marine is a full-service marine service provider with locations on Smith Creek, the Shinnecock Canal, and Moriches Bay. Our certified marine technicians, diesel mechanics, and WaveRunner specialists offer expert boat maintenance, engine diagnostics, fiberglass repair, winterization, shrink wrapping, and more to keep your vessel performing at its best.

Storage Options

Keep your vessel protected year-round with seasonal boat storage and winterization services. From shrink wrapping and engine preparation to battery maintenance and off-season care, our team helps keep your boat in peak condition with both indoor and outdoor storage options available.

Our Marinas

Located on Moriches Bay, Smith Creek, and the Shinnecock Canal, our full-service Long Island marinas provide premium dockage, marine services, and direct access to the waters of the Hamptons and Eastern Long Island.

Shinnecock Canal in Hamptons Bays

Situated on the Shinnecock Canal, our flagship 6.5-acre marina offers premium dockage for boats up to 82 feet, high-and-dry valet service, professional marine amenities, and easy access to the on-site Canal Café restaurant.

Smith Creek in Hampton Bays

Located near Shinnecock Bay, this full-service Hampton Bays marina offers in-water dockage, expert marine service from factory-trained technicians, and secure indoor and outdoor winter storage solutions.

Moriches Bay in Eastport/Remsenburg

Our Moriches Bay marina offers convenient in-water dockage, high-and-dry storage, ValvTect marine gasoline, and comprehensive marine services backed by factory-trained technicians and modern haul-out equipment.
