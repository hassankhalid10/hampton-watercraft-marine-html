Marina & Dockage Services

Direct Moriches Bay access with deep-water dockage, high-and-dry storage, and a full-service marina built around easy on-water living:

  • In-Water Slips: Seasonal and transient dockage for a wide range of vessel sizes.
  • High-and-Dry Storage: Fast-launch rack storage to keep your boat clean and protected.
  • Winter Storage: Secure outdoor storage with shrink-wrapping and engine prep.
  • Service Department: Mercury CSI-recognized technicians and full mobile service coverage.
