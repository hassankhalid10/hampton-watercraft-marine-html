A Higher Standard of Boating Experience

The Legacy of Hampton Watercraft & Marine

What began as a passion for the water in 1990 has evolved into Eastern Long Island’s premier marine destination. Founded by Tony Villareale, and later joined by his brother Joe, Hampton Watercraft & Marine was built on a simple yet powerful realization: the East End deserved a dealership that paired world-class inventory with a sophisticated, service-first approach. Starting as a small operation, we have grown into a multi-location powerhouse, expanding from our original roots to include three state-of-the-art marinas and a flagship showroom that serves as the heartbeat of the local boating community.

Our Culture

“100% Satisfaction, No Excuses”

At Hampton Watercraft & Marine, our culture is built around a simple promise: “100% Customer Satisfaction, No Excuses.” Since 1990, we’ve combined premium boat sales, factory-trained service, and customer-first support to deliver an unmatched marine experience across Eastern Long Island. From our knowledgeable staff to our state-of-the-art marina facilities, every part of our operation is centered around quality, transparency, and long-term relationships within the boating community.

  • Expertise: We invest heavily in our people, boasting factory-trained technicians with elite certifications, including Mercury V12 specialization.
  • Integrity: We treat every vessel as if it were our own, whether it’s a high-performance Tiara or a family-friendly Boston Whaler.
  • Community: We don’t just sell boats; we foster a community through local fishing tournaments, “Take A Vet Fishing” days, and seasonal rendezvous.
