at Hampton Watercraft & Marine

Hampton Watercraft & Marine is an award-winning marine dealership representing Tiara Yachts, Boston Whaler, Limestone, BRIG, Albemarle, Key West Boats, and NAVAN Boats, along with Mercury and Yamaha Outboards and MerCruiser and Volvo engines. With three full-service marinas located in Hampton Bays and Eastport, we continue to grow our team with talented, reliable, and motivated individuals who are passionate about the boating industry and customer service excellence.

Hampton Watercraft & Marine is a growing company that recognizes our employees as one of our greatest strengths. We are looking for motivated, dependable, and detail-oriented individuals to join our team and help deliver exceptional customer service across our marine sales, service, and marina operations. We proudly support our team through ongoing training opportunities, professional development, and a comprehensive benefits package.
