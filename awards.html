What Our Awards Mean for You

Industry-Recognized Service

Our awards are earned through rigorous evaluation of customer satisfaction, service quality, and operational excellence.

Trusted Marine Expertise

These recognitions reflect the high level of customer satisfaction, marine service quality, and boating support our team provides.

Commitment to Quality

Each award reflects our commitment to exceptional customer service, unmatched marine support, and a premium boating experience on Long Island.

Boating Industry Top 100

Recognized as one of the top 100 dealers in the nation by Boating Industry magazine, showcasing our commitment to excellence in sales, service, and customer satisfaction.

Mercury Service CSI Award

Honored for outstanding customer service excellence in Mercury Marine service operations for six consecutive years. Awarded for consistently delivering trusted Mercury Marine repair, maintenance, and customer support services to boaters throughout Long Island.

Boston Whaler Outstanding Sales & CSI Award

Proud recipient of Boston Whaler’s Customer Satisfaction Index Award for 2024. This recognition reflects our commitment to providing an outstanding boating experience backed by responsive service and customer care.

NMMA CSI Award

Recipients of the Customer Satisfaction Index Award issued on behalf of the National Marine Manufacturers Association for the years 2017, 2020, 2021, and 2023.

Tiara Yachts Elite Service Dealer

Proudly recognized as the only Tiara dealer to achieve Elite Status across all four reporting segments in 2024, setting the benchmark for service excellence within the Tiara network.

Key West Boats CSI Award

Awarded by Key West Boats for delivering exceptional customer service and trusted support for Key West boat owners.

Marine Certified Dealer

Certified as a Marine Industry dealer meeting rigorous standards for business practices, customer service, and professional operations excellence.

Brunswick Five Star Dealer

Achieved the prestigious Five Star Dealer status from Brunswick Corporation, recognizing excellence across all aspects of dealership operations, customer experience, and brand representation.
