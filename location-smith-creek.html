Expert Marine Service

Smith Creek is our dedicated service-first location, with a team of factory-trained technicians who handle everything from routine maintenance to major repairs:

  • Engine Service: Mercury, Yamaha, and Volvo Penta certified technicians.
  • Fiberglass & Paint: Awlgrip refinishing, gelcoat restoration, and structural repair.
  • Winterization: Full winterization, shrink-wrapping, and engine prep services.
  • Mobile Service: Dockside service across Hampton Bays and surrounding areas.

In-Water Dockage & Storage

Quiet, protected dockage on Smith Creek with secure indoor and outdoor winter storage solutions for boats of every size:

  • Seasonal Slips: Sheltered in-water slips with easy access to Shinnecock Bay.
  • Indoor Storage: Climate-controlled indoor winter storage to protect your investment.
  • Outdoor Storage: Secure outdoor storage with shrink-wrapping options.
  • Haul-Out: Modern haul-out equipment for safe launch and retrieval.
